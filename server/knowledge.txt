## English Q&A

Q: Mam will this also scan tags without qr code or bar code?
A: Yes, it will scan all jewellery with or without barcodes and QR codes. It directly scans the text written on the tag.

Q: Mam will scanner also do calculations for 14k & 18k also?
A: Yes, this scanning software can calculate both 14k and 18k gold.

Q: Mam will this detect and calculate on basis of Live rates of MCX ?
A: Yes, it continuously monitors live rates and calculates based on MCX live rates.
